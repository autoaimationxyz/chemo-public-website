SECTION 04
About Chemotech
CHEMOTECH is a young team of engineers and professionals
who supply, install, commission and maintain electrical, power,
automation and water-treatment systems. We have earned a
reputation for professionalism while working with organisations
of repute.
Since 2014-15, CHEMOTECH has evolved into a stronger and more focused firm,
serving industry, infrastructure and government projects across India, Nepal and
Bhutan. Our team brings a vast range of experience across electrical, power,
automation, water and building systems, and is always looking for new challenges.
Ours is a unique combination of products and services under one roof. Working with
leading partner brands, we recommend the right equipment for each requirement and
take responsibility for the complete job – from selection and supply to installation,
commissioning and after-sales support – giving our clients a single point of
responsibility.
We are passionate about the quality of work we do. This is one of the reasons, we
believe, that our clients have entrusted us with more and more challenging assignments
over the years. Our consultative approach – guided by the client's site conditions,
specifications and budget – is time-tested and effective. Our most successful projects
are usually collaborative efforts, in which the CHEMOTECH team and the client work
closely together, producing practical, reliable and result-oriented solutions.
CHEMOTECH is tough under pressure and committed to timelines. You can rest
assured that everything – from requirement study and design, sourcing and supply to
installation, commissioning and maintenance – will be handled seamlessly.
